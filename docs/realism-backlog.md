| 1 | Hands-off at trimmed cruise, trainer/sport settle into a 20–28° left bank; the wing reaches ~50° after 30 s. Partly addressed 2026-09-25 (slipstream implementation): slipstream swirl is now modelled and the motor reaction on the airframe no longer includes the rotor friction. Bank after 30 s: sport −28.0° → −26.4°, trainer −21.7° → −19.9° (10 s: −25.4° → −23.2°, −15.8° → −13.6°). The remaining bank cannot be attributed yet (see the next column). | No fuselage/high-wing dihedral effect; roll-due-to-yaw-rate overstated by strip model (~CL/2.9 vs DATCOM ~CL/4). Swirl is not testable with the current calibration: `PropWash.SwirlEfficiency` is set for a static hover and scales the swirl field to about 1/6 of the physical swirl angle, so its effect in cruise is understated. Friction in the reaction torque was a small part (fixed). | Hands-off logs from the real trainer. |
| 2 | Trainer pitches up strongly with full power. Checked 2026-09-25 with the physical prop wash (contraction, mixing, momentum flux = thrust): the prop-wash hypothesis does not hold. The pitch moment change from cruise to full throttle, frozen at 15 m/s, is small in both models: −0.02 N·m before, +0.08 N·m now (1.4% of full up elevator); at 10 m/s and 5° alpha, −0.06 → +0.12 N·m. The 2° down thrust (−0.47 N·m at full thrust) roughly cancels the tail download in the wash. The pitch attitude 3 s after going from cruise to full throttle is 16° higher than at cruise throttle (15° before): the climb response of a speed-stable aircraft with T/W ≈ 1, not a trim change. | Probably realistic: an overpowered, speed-stable trainer climbs when the throttle is opened. | Real full-throttle step from trimmed cruise with the stick fixed; compare the attitude 3 s later with `HoverTests.Report_slipstream_effects(trainer)`. |
| 3 | Mostly resolved 2026-09-25: the flying wing now flies at a static margin of 7.7% MAC (real wings fly at 5–10%) with the `cg` at 0.31 m behind the nose (was 0.32, +3.4%) and 6° washout (was 2°); it trims at 13 m/s with elevator −0.044. In a slow pull to the stall the uncommanded roll peaks at 49°/s (44°/s before the 2026-09-25 winglet change, #14; was 123°/s; asserted < 60°/s by `WingPitchStabilityTests.Wing_slow_pull_to_stall_does_not_roll_off`). Residual: the stall roll is CG sensitive. With −6° it stays below 60°/s only from cg x 0.308 to 0.312 (±2 mm); 0.307 gives 86°/s, 0.306 100°/s, 0.313 83°/s, 0.315 66°/s. With −5° only 0.309–0.310 passed (0.311 gave 171°/s). −7° holds 0.307–0.315 but needs trim elevator −0.08 to −0.13. The strip model's abrupt post-stall polars and the lack of spanwise flow may exaggerate this; treat the stall-departure numbers as qualitative. The hand-launch test now uses a closed-loop pilot (climb attitude + wings level). Also remaining: 0.15 elevator still gives +8° alpha (36°/s), since ±20° elevon throws are large for a 1.1 m wing (real setups use about ±10–12° with expo); since the 2026-09-25 yaw change (#14) the aileron share is ±12°, the pitch share still ±20°. | Before: CG estimate too far aft; the open-loop launch test tied the CG to the test. | Measure the real airframe's CG, washout and elevon throws; compare a stick-step pitch response from telemetry with `WingPitchStabilityTests.Report_wing_handling`. |

| 10 | Flying wing rolls to knife-edge at full throttle hands-off. Unchanged by the 2026-09-25 slipstream work: the pusher prop sits behind the wing, so no surface is in its swirl, and removing the rotor friction from the reaction torque changes the bank after 5 s at full throttle from 14 m/s only from −83.0° to −82.8°. | Motor reaction torque with no fin/slipstream counter-moment; low roll damping. | Real full-throttle hands-off logs; check motor torque and roll damping. |
| 11 | Sport full-aileron roll helix angle pb/2V is 0.227 at 18 m/s (391°/s) since 2026-09-25, converged in the strip count with fractional control coverage (it was 0.245, 421°/s, with 6 strips and centre assignment, which ran the aileron to the tip; the trainer went from 205 to 166°/s and the wing from 228 to 206°/s for the same reason) with the DATCOM plain-flap large-deflection correction (0.361 without it); RC aerobatic models reach about 0.12–0.18. Throw is no longer the lever: K'·δ plateaus above ~18°, and 15° still gave 0.218 (before 2026-09-25). `RollRateTests.Sport_full_aileron_roll_helix_angle_is_realistic` is skipped until this is resolved. | Ailerons span 8–95% of the half-span (from the fuselage side since 2026-09-25, 15–95% before; large for a sport model); thin-airfoil flap effectiveness × 0.85 may be optimistic for a thick section at low Reynolds number (DATCOM's cl_δ/cl_δ,theory correction, Figure 6.1.1.1-39B, is not modeled); roll damping from the strip model may be low. | Real roll-rate log at full throw; measure the real aileron span and chord; compare Cl_p and Cl_δ with VSPAERO. |
| 12 | Prop hang without trim: the sport's closed-loop hover (`Hover.Fly`, PD attitude pilot) only holds when the pilot holds the static hover trim. Without it the rudder saturates 42% of the time and the aircraft is lost at 5.7 s: the PD loop holds the yaw bias (mostly the 2° right thrust, −0.34 N·m in a hover) with a steady tilt, the aircraft drifts, and the fin in the slipstream weathercocks the nose into the drift. The 3D (0° right thrust) does not need it. | Real effect of a tail-sitter with a fin in the slipstream, made worse by the right thrust in a hover; the pilot model has no integral action. | Real prop hang: how much rudder the sport needs and how fast it drifts; a pilot model with integral action and latency. |
| 13 | The slipstream is not bent by crossflow or sideslip (it always follows the thrust axis), and the induced velocity ignores the crossflow (momentum theory on the axial inflow only, not Glauert's total inflow √((V_axial + v_i)² + V_cross²)). | Simplifications of `PropWash`. | Compare fin and stab loads in a sideslipping or high-alpha climb with VSPAERO or flight data. |
| 14 | Flying wing "a bit unstable in yaw" (user, 2026-09-25): a lightly damped, mostly-yaw Dutch roll, plus 5–13° of adverse sideslip after every roll input because the wing trims at α ≈ 6°. Mostly addressed 2026-09-25 with estimated data (approved by the user): winglets 0.12 → 0.135 m span and 3 cm aft, elevon aileron throw ±20° → ±12° through the mix. 0.3 aileron for 1 s now gives 5.8° peak sideslip (was 12.5°), Cnβ 0.055 (was 0.038), Cnr −0.025 (was −0.016), Dutch-roll ζωn 0.58 1/s (was 0.46), ζ 0.116 (was 0.106); asserted by `WingYawTests.Wing_roll_input_does_not_make_the_nose_snake`. Residual: ζ stays near 0.1 (it grows only as about √ winglet size); the approved 0.14 m winglets fail the slow stall pull (75°/s), so that test caps the winglet size; full-aileron pb/2V is now 0.096 at 14 m/s, low for an RC wing (0.12–0.18). | Small winglets on a short arm (tail volume 0.015); the strip model also lacks the wing's CL² sweep Cnβ (+0.006), fuller swept-wing Cnr, the pusher-prop normal force and rotational turbulence (docs/investigations/2026-09-25-wing-yaw.md, fix 3). | Measure the real winglets and elevon throws; compare a telemetry aileron pulse (sideslip needs a vane or an estimate from yaw rate and lateral acceleration) with `WingYawTests.Report_wing_yaw_characteristics`; check the derivatives with VSPAERO. |
